## Application 1 | 应用 1

### 1.1 Application Name | 应用名称

Helios Collect: AI Receivables Agent

### 1.2 Financial Problem | 金融问题

Small businesses often wait weeks or months to be paid for work they have already delivered. Collecting that money takes ongoing effort: tracking what is owed, matching payments to invoices, following up, and handling the replies that come back, such as promises to pay later, requests to split a payment, partial payments, disputes or no response at all. This work is time-consuming and easy to put off, so overdue payments build up and money the business has earned stays out of reach, putting pressure on its cash flow.

### 1.3 Target Users | 目标用户

Small and medium businesses that invoice customers, sell on payment terms, take deposits or bill in stages, across industries such as wholesale and distribution, food and events, professional services, construction and manufacturing. Day-to-day users are business owners, finance or office staff, and bookkeepers, including bookkeepers who manage multiple client businesses.

### 1.4 Core Features | 核心功能

- **Receivables overview:** see every invoice, deposit and amount owed, with its status, in one place.
- **Payment matching:** incoming payments are matched to the right invoices, including partial or unclear payments, with uncertain matches sent to the user to confirm.
- **Automatic follow-up:** the agent follows up with customers on a schedule the user sets and adjusts its approach as time passes.
- **Understanding replies:** the agent reads customer replies, tracks promises to pay, follows up when promises are missed, and agrees payment arrangements within the user's limits.
- **User-defined rules and approvals:** the user sets rules in plain language, such as how often to follow up or what concessions are allowed. Anything outside those rules is sent to the user for approval.
- **Dispute handling:** follow-up pauses when a customer raises a dispute, and the case is escalated to the user.
- **Activity history:** every message, decision and approval is recorded.

Users can set up their collection approach in plain language, see who owes what and why, approve or reject the agent's proposals, and customize the app to how their own business collects payment.

### 1.5 Innovation & Value | 创新与价值

**Innovation:** Helios Collect is a long-running agent that manages the full follow-up process until payment arrives, rather than sending fixed reminders. It understands customer replies and adapts its actions, while every action is checked by the Helios platform against rules the user has approved, so the agent cannot act outside its authority. The same app can be adapted to different businesses and billing styles by describing them in natural language, without changing the platform.

**Practical value:** businesses get paid sooner, spend less time chasing payments, and keep follow-up consistent and professional, which protects customer relationships.

**Differentiation:** typical invoicing tools offer fixed reminder templates inside a single system. Helios Collect handles the whole conversation, fits each business's own process, and operates under platform-enforced rules rather than relying on the AI's own judgment.

### 1.6 User Attraction | 用户吸引力

Users choose Helios Collect because its value is direct and easy to measure: money comes in sooner with less effort. It is quick to set up in plain language and safe to adopt, since users can start by approving every action and give the agent more independence as trust grows. Once a business's customers, invoices and rules are on Helios, adding other Helios apps is a natural next step. Bookkeepers and advisors can roll it out across many clients, and businesses can share and reuse versions suited to their industry, bringing more users to the platform.

## Application 2 | 应用 2

### 2.1 Application Name | 应用名称

Helios Payables: AI Payables Agent

### 2.2 Financial Problem | 金融问题

Paying suppliers on time is largely manual. Invoices arrive through different channels, due dates are easy to miss, and when a payment is due the money is often not in the right account. Late payments damage supplier relationships and can lead to penalties or lost discounts. Rushed, manual payments also create risk: the same invoice can be paid twice, or money can be sent to a fraudster posing as a supplier with new bank details. Once money leaves the business, it is often difficult or impossible to recover.

### 2.3 Target Users | 目标用户

Small and medium businesses that regularly pay suppliers, subcontractors or freelancers, across industries such as wholesale, food service, construction, professional services and manufacturing. Day-to-day users are business owners, finance staff and bookkeepers.

### 2.4 Core Features | 核心功能

- **Invoice intake:** supplier invoices are collected from email or upload, and key details such as supplier, amount, due date and payment details are extracted.
- **Bills calendar:** see all upcoming payments and due dates in one place.
- **Funding and money movement:** before a payment is due, the agent checks the business's account balances and moves money between the business's own accounts when needed so bills can be paid on time.
- **On-time payment:** bills are paid on their due dates, and each payment is made exactly once.
- **Safety checks:** duplicate invoices, changes to a supplier's payment details and unusual amounts are held for the user to verify.
- **User-defined rules and approvals:** the user sets rules in plain language, such as which payments can be made automatically, minimum balances to keep, and which payments or transfers need approval.
- **Audit trail:** every transfer, payment, hold and approval is recorded with its reason.

Users can submit invoices, see what is due and whether funds are available, approve held payments or transfers, set payment rules in plain language, and customize the app to their own payment process.

### 2.5 Innovation & Value | 创新与价值

**Innovation:** Helios Payables is an agent that can move money and pay bills while remaining unable to misuse funds. Every transfer and payment passes through the Helios platform, which enforces the user's rules independently of the AI, so even if the AI is misled, unauthorized payments are refused. AI is used to read invoices and messages, while amounts, balances and safety checks are handled by deterministic rules. The app can be adapted to each business's payment process through natural language.

**Practical value:** suppliers are paid on time, owners stop paying bills by hand, and duplicate or fraudulent payments are stopped before money leaves the business.

**Differentiation:** typical bill-pay tools are tied to one system and rely on users or AI to notice problems. Helios Payables actively manages money across the business's own accounts and runs under rules enforced by the platform, so safety does not depend on the AI catching every issue.

### 2.6 User Attraction | 用户吸引力

Users choose Helios Payables because it saves time every week, keeps supplier relationships strong, and protects the business from costly payment mistakes. Businesses can start with approval required for every payment and automate gradually as they gain confidence. It shares data with other Helios apps, giving businesses one view of money coming in and going out, and it shows owners who are cautious about AI handling money that they stay in control on Helios, which encourages them to bring more of their financial work onto the platform.

## Application 3 | 应用 3

### 3.1 Application Name | 应用名称

Helios Cash Planner: AI Cash Flow Agent

### 3.2 Financial Problem | 金融问题

Small business owners often discover a cash shortage only when payroll or a major bill is already due. Typical forecasts assume customers pay on time, but in reality payments arrive late, in parts or after repeated promises. Many owners rely on spreadsheets or rough estimates, so they cannot see problems coming weeks ahead or judge the effect of decisions such as accepting a large order on long payment terms. When a shortfall arrives, they are left with costly last-minute options.

### 3.3 Target Users | 目标用户

Owners, managers and bookkeepers of small and medium businesses, especially those that sell on payment terms, have seasonal or uneven income, or operate with limited cash reserves. It is also intended for accountants and financial advisors who support multiple small business clients.

### 3.4 Core Features | 核心功能

- **Rolling cash forecast:** a forecast of the weeks ahead that combines current balances, expected customer payments, upcoming supplier payments and regular costs, based on how customers actually pay rather than only on due dates.
- **Early warning:** alerts the user well in advance when cash is expected to fall below a level they set.
- **Action plan:** recommends practical steps, such as which customers to follow up with or which payments could be rescheduled. Approved actions are carried out through other Helios apps under their rules; the planner itself never moves money.
- **What-if scenarios:** users ask questions in plain language, such as the effect of a customer paying late or accepting a new order on different terms.
- **Market data insights:** uses public financial market data through Helios data services, such as borrowing costs, to help users compare options and understand risks.
- **Regular briefings:** short summaries of the cash position, risks and recommended actions.

Users can see whether they will have enough cash in the coming weeks, explore scenarios, review and approve recommended actions, and set their own safety levels and planning preferences.

### 3.5 Innovation & Value | 创新与价值

**Innovation:** Helios Cash Planner forecasts based on real payment behaviour, learning from the activity captured by other Helios apps, so its forecasts reflect what is likely to happen rather than what invoices say. It combines a business's own data with public financial market data, giving small businesses a level of planning usually available only to larger companies. It also shows how apps on Helios work together: it reads shared financial data, recommends actions, and passes approved actions to other apps, where platform-enforced rules still apply. Planning periods, safety levels and business patterns can be customized through natural language.

**Practical value:** owners get time to act before a problem becomes a crisis, rely less on emergency borrowing and extra fees, and make better-informed decisions about orders, payment terms and spending.

**Differentiation:** most small business forecasts are spreadsheets or static projections. Helios Cash Planner updates continuously, reflects real customer behaviour, draws on market data, explains its reasoning in plain language, and turns warnings into actions that run under platform-enforced rules.

### 3.6 User Attraction | 用户吸引力

Users choose Helios Cash Planner because it answers the question every owner worries about: whether there will be enough cash in the weeks ahead. It requires no spreadsheet work, since the forecast builds itself from data already on Helios. Businesses that use other Helios apps get more accurate forecasts automatically, which encourages them to bring more of their finances onto the platform. Accountants and advisors can monitor cash across all their clients, bringing many businesses to Helios at once, and the app demonstrates how each new app on Helios makes the others more useful.
